# Riveted Joints — Practice Exercises

## Exercise 1
A single riveted lap joint is made in $15\text{ mm}$ thick plates with $20\text{ mm}$ diameter rivets. Determine the strength of the joint, if the pitch of rivets is $60\text{ mm}$. Take $\sigma_t = 120\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 90\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 160\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $28\,280\text{ N}$

## Exercise 2
Two plates $16\text{ mm}$ thick are joined by a double riveted lap joint. The pitch of each row of rivets is $90\text{ mm}$. The rivets are $25\text{ mm}$ in diameter. The permissible stresses are as follows: $\sigma_t = 140\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 110\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 240\text{ MPa}$. Find the efficiency of the joint.
> **Ans:** $53.5\%$

## Exercise 3
A single riveted double cover butt joint is made in $10\text{ mm}$ thick plates with $20\text{ mm}$ diameter rivets with a pitch of $60\text{ mm}$. Calculate the efficiency of the joint, if $\sigma_t = 100\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 80\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 160\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $53.8\%$

## Exercise 4
A double riveted double cover butt joint is made in $12\text{ mm}$ thick plates with $18\text{ mm}$ diameter rivets. Find the efficiency of the joint for a pitch of $80\text{ mm}$, if $\sigma_t = 115\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 80\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 160\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $62.6\%$

## Exercise 5
A double riveted lap joint with chain riveting is to be made for joining two plates $10\text{ mm}$ thick. The allowable stresses are: $\sigma_t = 60\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 50\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 80\text{ MPa}$. Find the rivet diameter, pitch of rivets and distance between rows of rivets. Also find the efficiency of the joint.
> **Ans:** $d = 20\text{ mm};\; p = 73\text{ mm};\; p_b = 38\text{ mm};\; \eta = 71.7\%$

## Exercise 6
A triple riveted lap joint with zig-zag riveting is to be designed to connect two plates of $6\text{ mm}$ thickness. Determine the diameter of rivet, pitch of rivets and distance between the rows of rivets. Indicate how the joint will fail. Assume: $\sigma_t = 120\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 100\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 150\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $d = 14\text{ mm};\; p = 78\text{ mm};\; p_b = 35.2\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 7
A double riveted butt joint, in which the pitch of the rivets in the outer rows is twice that in the inner rows, connects two $16\text{ mm}$ thick plates with two cover plates each $12\text{ mm}$ thick. The diameter of rivets is $22\text{ mm}$. Determine the pitches of the rivets in the two rows if the working stresses are not to exceed the following limits: Tensile stress in plates $= 100\text{ MPa}$; Shear stress in rivets $= 75\text{ MPa}$; and bearing stress in rivets and plates $= 150\text{ MPa}$. Make a fully dimensioned sketch of the joint by showing at least two views.
> **Ans:** $107\text{ mm},\; 53.5\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 8
Design a double riveted double strap butt joint for the longitudinal seam of a boiler shell, $750\text{ mm}$ in diameter, to carry a maximum steam pressure of $1.05\text{ N/mm}^2$ gauge. The allowable stresses are: $\sigma_t = 35\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 28\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 52.5\text{ MPa}$. Assume the efficiency of the joint as $75\%$.
> **Ans:** $t = 16\text{ mm};\; d = 25\text{ mm};\; p = 63\text{ mm};\; p_b = 37.5\text{ mm};\; t_1 = t_2 = 10\text{ mm};\; m = 37.5\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 9
Design a triple riveted double strap butt joint with chain riveting for a boiler of $1.5\text{ m}$ diameter and carrying a pressure of $1.2\text{ N/mm}^2$. The allowable stresses are: $\sigma_t = 105\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 77\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 162.5\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $d = 20\text{ mm};\; p = 50\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 10
Design a triple riveted longitudinal double strap butt joint with unequal straps for a boiler. The inside diameter of the longest course of the drum is $1.3\text{ metres}$. The joint is to be designed for a steam pressure of $2.4\text{ N/mm}^2$. The working stresses to be used are: $\sigma_t = 77\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 62\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 120\text{ MPa}$. Assume the efficiency of the joint as $81\%$.
> **Ans:** $t = 26\text{ mm};\; d = 31.5\text{ mm};\; p = 200\text{ mm};\; t_1 = 19.5\text{ mm};\; t_2 = 16.5\text{ mm};\; m = 47.5\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 11
Design the longitudinal and circumferential joint for a boiler whose diameter is $2.4\text{ metres}$ and is subjected to a pressure of $1\text{ N/mm}^2$. The longitudinal joint is a triple riveted butt joint with an efficiency of about $85\%$ and the circumferential joint is a double riveted lap joint with an efficiency of about $70\%$. The pitch in the outer rows of the rivets is to be double than in the inner rows and the width of the cover plates is unequal. The allowable stresses are: $\sigma_t = 77\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 56\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 120\text{ MPa}$. Assume that the resistance of rivets in double shear is $1.875$ times that of single shear. Draw the complete joint.

## Exercise 12
A triple riveted butt joint with equal double cover plates (zig-zag riveting) is used for the longitudinal joint of a Lancashire boiler of $2.5\text{ m}$ internal diameter. The working steam pressure is $1.12\text{ N/mm}^2$ and the efficiency of the joint is $85\%$. Calculate the plate thickness for mild steel of $460\text{ MPa}$ ultimate tensile strength. Assume ratio of tensile to shear stresses as $7/6$ and factor of safety $4$. The resistance of the rivets in double shear is to be taken as $1.875$ times that of single shear. Design a suitable circumferential joint also.

## Exercise 13
Two lengths of mild steel flat tie bars $200\text{ mm} \times 10\text{ mm}$ are to be connected by a double riveted double cover butt joint, using $24\text{ mm}$ diameter rivets. Design the joint, if the allowable working stresses are $112\text{ MPa}$ in tension, $84\text{ MPa}$ in shear and $200\text{ MPa}$ in crushing.
> **Ans:** $n = 5;\; \eta = 88\%$

## Exercise 14
Two mild steel tie bars for a bridge structure are to be joined by a double cover butt joint. The thickness of the tie bar is $20\text{ mm}$ and carries a tensile load of $400\text{ kN}$. Design the joint if the allowable stresses are: $\sigma_t = 90\text{ MPa}$, $\tau = 75\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_c = 150\text{ MPa}$. Assume the strength of rivet in double shear to be $1.75$ times that of in single shear.
> **Ans:** $b = 150\text{ mm};\; d = 27\text{ mm};\; n = 6;\; \eta = 90\%$

## Exercise 15
Two lengths of mild steel tie rod having width $200\text{ mm}$ are to be connected by means of Lozenge joint with two cover plates to withstand a tensile load of $180\text{ kN}$. Completely design the joint, if the permissible stresses are $80\text{ MPa}$ in tension, $65\text{ MPa}$ in shear and $160\text{ MPa}$ in crushing. Draw a neat sketch of the joint.
> **Ans:** $t = 13\text{ mm};\; d = 22\text{ mm};\; n = 5;\; \eta = 86.5\%$

## Exercise 17
A bracket is riveted to a column by $6$ rivets of equal size as shown in Fig. 9.38. It carries a load of $100\text{ kN}$ at a distance of $250\text{ mm}$ from the column. If the maximum shear stress in the rivet is limited to $63\text{ MPa}$, find the diameter of the rivet.

## Exercise 18
A bracket in the form of a plate is fitted to a column by means of four rivets of the same size, as shown in Fig. 9.39. A load of $100\text{ kN}$ is applied to the bracket at an angle of $60^\circ$ to the horizontal and the line of action of the load passes through the centre of the bottom rivet. If the maximum shear stress for the material of the rivet is $70\text{ MPa}$, find the diameter of rivets. What will be the thickness of the plate if the crushing stress is $100\text{ MPa}$?
> **Ans:** $29\text{ mm};\; 1.5\text{ mm}$

# Shafts — Practice Exercises

## Exercise 1
A shaft running at $400\text{ r.p.m.}$ transmits $10\text{ kW}$. Assuming allowable shear stress in shaft as $40\text{ MPa}$, find the diameter of the shaft.
> **Ans:** $35\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 2
A hollow steel shaft transmits $600\text{ kW}$ at $500\text{ r.p.m.}$ The maximum shear stress is $62.4\text{ MPa}$. Find the outside and inside diameter of the shaft, if the outer diameter is twice of inside diameter, assuming that the maximum torque is $20\%$ greater than the mean torque.
> **Ans:** $100\text{ mm};\; 50\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 3
A hollow shaft for a rotary compressor is to be designed to transmit a maximum torque of $4750\text{ N-m}$. The shear stress in the shaft is limited to $50\text{ MPa}$. Determine the inside and outside diameters of the shaft, if the ratio of the inside to the outside diameter is $0.4$.
> **Ans:** $35\text{ mm};\; 90\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 4
A motor car shaft consists of a steel tube $30\text{ mm}$ internal diameter and $4\text{ mm}$ thick. The engine develops $10\text{ kW}$ at $2000\text{ r.p.m.}$ Find the maximum shear stress in the tube when the power is transmitted through a $4 : 1$ gearing.
> **Ans:** $30\text{ MPa}$

## Exercise 5
A cylindrical shaft made of steel of yield strength $700\text{ MPa}$ is subjected to static loads consisting of a bending moment of $10\text{ kN-m}$ and a torsional moment of $30\text{ kN-m}$. Determine the diameter of the shaft using two different theories of failure and assuming a factor of safety of $2$.
> **Ans:** $100\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 6
A line shaft rotating at $200\text{ r.p.m.}$ is to transmit $20\text{ kW}$. The allowable shear stress for the material of the shaft is $42\text{ MPa}$. If the shaft carries a central load of $900\text{ N}$ and is simply supported between bearing $3\text{ metre}$ apart, determine the diameter of the shaft. The maximum tensile or compressive stress is not to exceed $56\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $50\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 7
Two $400\text{ mm}$ diameter pulleys are keyed to a simply supported shaft $500\text{ mm}$ apart. Each pulley is $100\text{ mm}$ from its support and has horizontal belts, tension ratio being $2.5$. If the shear stress is to be limited to $80\text{ MPa}$ while transmitting $45\text{ kW}$ at $900\text{ r.p.m.}$, find the shaft diameter if it is to be used for the input-output belts being on the same or opposite sides.
> **Ans:** $40\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 8
A cast gear wheel is driven by a pinion and transmits $100\text{ kW}$ at $375\text{ r.p.m.}$ The gear has $200\text{ machine cut}$ teeth having $20^\circ$ pressure angle and is mounted at the centre of a $0.4\text{ m}$ long shaft. The gear weighs $2000\text{ N}$ and its pitch circle diameter is $1.2\text{ m}$. Design the gear shaft. Assume that the axes of the gear and pinion lie in the same horizontal plane.
> **Ans:** $80\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 9
Fig. 14.17 shows a shaft from a hand-operated machine. The frictional torque in the journal bearings at $A$ and $B$ is $15\text{ N-m}$ each. Find the diameter ($d$) of the shaft (on which the pulley is mounted) using maximum distortion energy criterion. The shaft material is 40 C 8 steel for which the yield stress in tension is $380\text{ MPa}$ and the factor of safety is $1.5$.
> **Ans:** $20\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 10
A line shaft is to transmit $30\text{ kW}$ at $160\text{ r.p.m.}$ It is driven by a motor placed directly under it by means of a belt running on a $1\text{ m}$ diameter pulley keyed to the end of the shaft. The tension in the tight side of the belt is $2.5$ times that in the slack side and the centre of the pulley over-hangs $150\text{ mm}$ beyond the centre line of the end bearing. Determine the diameter of the shaft, if the allowable shear stress is $56\text{ MPa}$ and the pulley weighs $1600\text{ N}$.
> **Ans:** $60\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 11
Determine the diameter of hollow shaft having inside diameter $0.5$ times the outside diameter. The permissible shear stress is limited to $200\text{ MPa}$. The shaft carries a $900\text{ mm}$ diameter cast iron pulley. This pulley is driven by another pulley mounted on the shaft placed below it. The belt ends are parallel and vertical. The ratio of tensions in the belt is $3$. The pulley on the hollow shaft weighs $800\text{ N}$ and overhangs the nearest bearing by $250\text{ mm}$. The pulley is to transmit $35\text{ kW}$ at $400\text{ r.p.m.}$
> **Ans:** $d_o = 40\text{ mm},\; d_i = 20\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 12
A horizontal shaft $AD$ supported in bearings at $A$ and $B$ and carrying pulleys at $C$ and $D$ is to transmit $75\text{ kW}$ at $500\text{ r.p.m.}$ from drive pulley $D$ to off-take pulley $C$, as shown in Fig. 14.18. Calculate the diameter of shaft. The data given is : $P_1 = 2 P_2$ (both horizontal), $Q_1 = 2 Q_2$ (both vertical), radius of pulley $C = 220\text{ mm}$, radius of pulley $D = 160\text{ mm}$, allowable shear stress $= 45\text{ MPa}$.
> **Ans:** $100\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 13
A line shaft $ABCD$, $9\text{ metres}$ long, has four pulleys $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ at equal distance apart. Power of $45\text{ kW}$ is being supplied to the shaft through the pulley $C$ while the power is being taken off equally from the pulleys $A$, $B$ and $D$. The shaft runs at $630\text{ r.p.m.}$
Calculate the most economical diameters for the various portions of the shaft so that the shear stress does not exceed $55\text{ MPa}$. If the shear modulus is $85\text{ GPa}$, determine the twist of the pulley $D$ with respect to the pulley $A$.
> **Ans:** $28\text{ mm},\; 36\text{ mm},\; 28\text{ mm};\; 0.0985^\circ$

## Exercise 14

The gear forces are as follows :
$F_t = 1590\text{ N}$; $F_r = 580\text{ N}$
Design the shaft diameter by maximum shear stress theory. The shaft material has the following properties :
Ultimate tensile strength $= 720\text{ MPa}$; Yield strength $= 380\text{ MPa}$; Factor of safety $= 1.5$.

## Exercise 15
An overhang hollow shaft carries a $900\text{ mm}$ diameter pulley, whose centre is $250\text{ mm}$ from the centre of the nearest bearing. The weight of the pulley is $600\text{ N}$ and the angle of lap is $180^\circ$. The pulley is driven by a motor vertically below it. If permissible tension in the belt is $2650\text{ N}$ and if coefficient of friction between the belt and pulley surface is $0.3$, estimate, diameters of shaft, when the internal diameter is $0.6$ of the external.
Neglect centrifugal tension and assume permissible tensile and shear stresses in the shaft as $84\text{ MPa}$ and $68\text{ MPa}$ respectively.
> **Ans:** $65\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 16
The shaft, as shown in Fig. 14.20, is driven by pulley $B$ from an electric motor. Another belt drive from pulley $A$ is running a compressor. The belt tensions for pulley $A$ are $1500\text{ N}$ and $600\text{ N}$. The ratio of belt tensions for pulley $B$ is $3.5$. The diameter of pulley $A$ is $150\text{ mm}$ and the diameter of pulley $B$ is $480\text{ mm}$. The allowable tensile stress for the shaft material is $170\text{ MPa}$ and the allowable shear stress is $85\text{ MPa}$. Taking torsion and bending factors as $1.25$ and $1.75$ respectively, find the shaft diameter.
Also find out the dimensions for a hollow shaft with outside diameter limited to $30\text{ mm}$. Compare the weights of the two shafts.

## Exercise 17
A mild steel shaft transmits $15\text{ kW}$ at $210\text{ r.p.m.}$ It is supported on two bearings $750\text{ mm}$ apart and has two gears keyed to it. The pinion having $24\text{ teeth}$ of $6\text{ mm}$ module is located $100\text{ mm}$ to the left of the right hand bearing and delivers the power horizontally to the right. The gear having $80\text{ teeth}$ of $6\text{ mm}$ module is located $15\text{ mm}$ to the right of the left hand bearing and receives power in a vertical direction from below. Assuming an allowable working shear stress as $53\text{ MPa}$, and a combined shock and fatigue factor of $1.5$ in bending as well as in torsion, determine the diameter of the shaft.
> **Ans:** $60\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 18
A steel shaft $800\text{ mm}$ long transmitting $15\text{ kW}$ at $400\text{ r.p.m.}$ is supported at two bearings at the two ends. A gear wheel having $80\text{ teeth}$ and $500\text{ mm}$ pitch circle diameter is mounted at $200\text{ mm}$ from the left hand side bearing and receives power from a pinion meshing with it. The axis of pinion and gear lie in the horizontal plane. A pulley of $300\text{ mm}$ diameter is mounted at $200\text{ mm}$ from right hand side bearing and is used for transmitting power by a belt. The belt drive is inclined at $30^\circ$ to the vertical in the forward direction. The belt lap angle is $180\text{ degrees}$. The coefficient of friction between belt and pulley is $0.3$. Design and sketch the arrangement of the shaft assuming the values of safe stresses as : $\tau = 55\text{ MPa}$; $\sigma_t = 80\text{ MPa}$. Take torsion and bending factor $1.5$ and $2$ respectively.
> **Ans:** $120\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 19

## Exercise 22

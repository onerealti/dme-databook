# Table 11.1: Design Dimensions of Screw Threads, Bolts and Nuts (IS: 4218 Part III - 1976)

> [!NOTE]
> In case the table is not available, the core diameter ($d_c$) may be taken as **$0.84 d$**, where $d$ is the major/nominal diameter (Core area $A_c \approx \frac{\pi}{4}(0.84 d)^2$).

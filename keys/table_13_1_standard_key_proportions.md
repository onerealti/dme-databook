### Key Formulas Summary
1. **Rectangular Sunk Key:** Width $w \approx \frac{d}{4}$, Thickness $t \approx \frac{d}{6}$
2. **Square Sunk Key:** Width $w = t \approx \frac{d}{4}$
3. **Keyway Effect on Shaft Strength:** $e = 1 - 0.2\left(\frac{w}{d}\right) - 1.1\left(\frac{h}{d}\right)$

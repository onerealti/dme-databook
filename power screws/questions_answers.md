## Question 1
**Differentiate between a differential screw and a compound screw.**

### Answer

| Feature | Differential Screw | Compound Screw |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Hand of Threads** | Both threaded portions have the **same hand** (e.g., both right-handed). | Threaded portions have **opposite hands** (one right-handed, one left-handed). |
| **Net Axial Motion per Turn** | $S = p_1 - p_2$ (Difference of pitches). | $S = p_1 + p_2$ (Sum of pitches). |
| **Velocity Ratio** | Extremely high mechanical advantage; moves the load by a very small distance per turn. | Moves the load quickly; used where fast opening/closing is required. |
| **Typical Applications** | Precision adjustment instruments, differential screw jacks, micrometers. | Quick-acting vices, turnbuckles, steam engine valve controls. |

### Reference Formula Summary

1. **Major Diameter Nut ($D$):**
   $$D = d + 0.5 \quad [\text{mm}]$$

2. **Minor / Core Diameter ($d_c$):**
   $$d_c = d - 2 h \quad [\text{mm}]$$

3. **Core Stress Area ($A_c$):**
   $$A_c = \frac{\pi}{4} (d_c)^2 \quad [\text{mm}^2]$$

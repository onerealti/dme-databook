# Shaft Couplings - Exercises

## Exercise 4
Design a muff coupling to connect two shafts transmitting $40\text{ kW}$ at $120\text{ rpm}$. The permissible shear and crushing stress for the shaft and key material (mild steel) are $30\text{ MPa}$ and $80\text{ MPa}$ respectively. The material of muff is cast iron with permissible shear stress of $15\text{ MPa}$. Assume that the maximum torque transmitted is $25\%$ greater than the mean torque.

> **Ans:** $d = 90\text{ mm}$, $w = 28\text{ mm}$, $t = 16\text{ mm}$, $l = 157.5\text{ mm}$; $D = 195\text{ mm}$, $L = 315\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 5
Design a compression coupling for a shaft to transmit $1300\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$. The allowable shear stress for the shaft and key is $40\text{ MPa}$ and the number of bolts connecting the two halves are $4$. The permissible tensile stress for the bolts material is $70\text{ MPa}$. The coefficient of friction between the muff and the shaft surface may be taken as $0.3$.

> **Ans:** $d = 55\text{ mm}$, $D = 125\text{ mm}$, $L = 192.5\text{ mm}$, $d_b = 24\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 6
Design a cast iron protective flange coupling to connect two shafts in order to transmit $7.5\text{ kW}$ at $720\text{ rpm}$.

The following permissible stresses may be used:
- **Permissible shear stress for shaft, bolt, and key material:** $33\text{ MPa}$
- **Permissible crushing stress for bolt and key material:** $60\text{ MPa}$
- **Permissible shear stress for cast iron:** $15\text{ MPa}$

> **Ans:** $d = 25\text{ mm}$, $D = 50\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 7
Two shafts made of plain carbon steel are connected by a rigid protective type flange coupling. The shafts are running at $500\text{ rpm}$ and transmit $25\text{ kW}$ power. Design the coupling completely for overload capacity $25\%$ in excess of mean transmitted torque capacity.

Assume the following permissible stresses for the coupling components:
- **Shaft:** Permissible tensile stress = $60\text{ MPa}$; Permissible shear stress = $35\text{ MPa}$
- **Keys:** Rectangular formed end sunk key having permissible compressive strength = $60\text{ MPa}$
- **Bolts:** Six numbers made of steel having permissible shear stress = $28\text{ MPa}$
- **Flanges:** Cast iron having permissible shear stress = $12\text{ MPa}$

Draw two views of the coupling you have designed.

> **Ans:** $d = 45\text{ mm}$, $D = 90\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 8
Design a shaft and flange for a Diesel engine in which protected type of flange coupling is to be adopted for power transmission.

The following data is available for design:
- **Power of engine:** $75\text{ kW}$
- **Speed of engine:** $200\text{ rpm}$
- **Maximum permissible stress in shaft:** $40\text{ MPa}$
- **Maximum permissible twist in shaft:** $1^\circ$ in length of shaft equal to $30$ times the diameter of shaft
- **Maximum torque:** $1.25 \times \text{mean torque}$
- **Pitch circle diameter of bolts:** $3 \times \text{diameter of shaft}$
- **Maximum permissible stress in bolts:** $20\text{ MPa}$

Find out:
1. Diameter of shaft
2. Number of bolts
3. Diameter of bolts

> **Ans:** $100\text{ mm}$, $4\text{ bolts}$, $22\text{ mm}$

## Exercise 9
A flanged protective type coupling is required to transmit $50\text{ kW}$ at $2000\text{ rpm}$. Find:

- (a) Shaft diameters if the driving shaft is hollow with $d_i / d_o = 0.6$ and driven shaft is a solid shaft. Take $\tau = 100\text{ MPa}$.
- (b) Diameter of bolts, if the coupling uses four bolts. Take $\sigma_c = \sigma_t = 70\text{ MPa}$ and $\tau = 25\text{ MPa}$. Assume pitch circle diameter as about $3$ times the outside diameter of the hollow shaft.
- (c) Thickness of the flange and diameter of the hub. Assume $\sigma_c = 100\text{ MPa}$ and $\tau = 125\text{ MPa}$.
- (d) Make a neat free hand sketch of the assembled coupling showing a longitudinal sectional elevation with the main dimensions. The other dimensions may be assumed suitably.

## Exercise 11
Design a bushed-pin type flexible coupling for connecting a motor shaft to a pump shaft for the following service conditions:
- **Power to be transmitted:** $40\text{ kW}$
- **Speed of the motor shaft:** $1000\text{ rpm}$
- **Diameter of the motor shaft:** $50\text{ mm}$
- **Diameter of the pump shaft:** $45\text{ mm}$

The bearing pressure in the rubber bush and allowable stress in the pins are to be limited to $0.45\text{ N/mm}^2$ and $25\text{ MPa}$ respectively.

> **Ans:** $d_1 = 20\text{ mm}$, $n = 6$, $d_2 = 40\text{ mm}$, $l = 152\text{ mm}$

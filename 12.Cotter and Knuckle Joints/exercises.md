# Cotter and Knuckle Joints — Practice Exercises

---

### Exercise 1

Design a cotter joint to connect two mild steel rods for a pull of $30\text{ kN}$. The maximum permissible stresses are $55\text{ MPa}$ in tension ($\sigma_t$), $40\text{ MPa}$ in shear ($\tau$), and $70\text{ MPa}$ in crushing ($\sigma_c$). Draw a neat sketch of the joint designed.

> **Answer:**  
> $d = 22\text{ mm}$; $d_2 = 32\text{ mm}$; $t = 14\text{ mm}$; $d_1 = 44\text{ mm}$; $b = 30\text{ mm}$; $a = 12\text{ mm}$; $d_4 = 65\text{ mm}$; $c = 12\text{ mm}$; $d_3 = 40\text{ mm}$; $t_1 = 8\text{ mm}$

---

### Exercise 2

Two rod ends of a pump are joined by means of a cotter and spigot and socket at the ends. Design the joint for an axial load of $100\text{ kN}$ which alternately changes from tensile to compressive. The allowable stresses for the material used are $50\text{ MPa}$ in tension, $40\text{ MPa}$ in shear, and $100\text{ MPa}$ in crushing.

> **Answer:**  
> $d = 51\text{ mm}$; $d_2 = 62\text{ mm}$; $t = 16\text{ mm}$; $d_1 = 72\text{ mm}$; $b = 78\text{ mm}$; $a = 20\text{ mm}$; $d_3 = 83\text{ mm}$; $d_4 = 125\text{ mm}$; $c = 16\text{ mm}$; $t_1 = 13\text{ mm}$

---

### Exercise 3

Two mild steel rods $40\text{ mm}$ diameter are to be connected by a cotter joint. The thickness of the cotter is $12\text{ mm}$. Calculate the dimensions of the joint, if the maximum permissible stresses are: $46\text{ MPa}$ in tension, $35\text{ MPa}$ in shear, and $70\text{ MPa}$ in crushing.

> **Answer:**  
> $d_2 = 30\text{ mm}$; $d_1 = 48\text{ mm}$; $b = 70\text{ mm}$; $a = 27.5\text{ mm}$; $d_4 = 100\text{ mm}$; $c = 12\text{ mm}$; $d_3 = 44.2\text{ mm}$; $t = 35\text{ mm}$; $t_1 = 13.5\text{ mm}$

### Exercise 7

Design a knuckle joint to connect two mild steel bars under a tensile load of $25\text{ kN}$. The allowable stresses are $65\text{ MPa}$ in tension, $50\text{ MPa}$ in shear, and $83\text{ MPa}$ in crushing.

> **Answer:**  
> $d = d_1 = 23\text{ mm}$; $d_2 = 46\text{ mm}$; $d_3 = 35\text{ mm}$; $t = 29\text{ mm}$; $t_1 = 18\text{ mm}$

### Exercise 8

A knuckle joint is required to withstand a tensile load of $25\text{ kN}$. Design the joint if the permissible stresses are: $\sigma_t = 56\text{ MPa}$; $\tau = 40\text{ MPa}$; and $\sigma_c = 70\text{ MPa}$.

> **Answer:**  
> $d = d_1 = 28\text{ mm}$; $d_2 = 56\text{ mm}$; $d_3 = 42\text{ mm}$; $t_1 = 21\text{ mm}$
